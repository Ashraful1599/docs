Create, train, test, and deploy your first AI Agent in about 5 minutes. This guide walks you through the ChatRos onboarding wizard and gets your assistant live on your website.

## Prerequisites

You'll need an active ChatRos account. [Sign up here](https://chatros.com/register) if you haven't already.

## Overview

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your agent">
    A short guided wizard sets up a new AI agent for your business.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add training data">
    Give the agent knowledge — files, text, your website, Q&A, or a product catalog.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test it">
    Chat with your agent to check its answers before going live.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deploy">
    Add it to your website (and any other channel) in a couple of clicks.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Estimated time:** ~5 minutes.

---

## Step 1 — Create your agent

From your dashboard, click **Create New Agent**.

<Frame caption="Your workspace's agents. Click “Create New Agent” to begin.">
  <img src="/images/user-guides/quick-start/first-agent/create-agent.png" alt="ChatRos agents page with the Create New Agent button" />
</Frame>

This opens a short **5‑step wizard**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Welcome">
    A quick intro showing your plan and how many agents it includes. Click **Let's go**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/user-guides/quick-start/first-agent/welcome.png" alt="Onboarding wizard welcome step" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell us about your business">
    Enter your **business name** and pick the type (Online shop, Services, Software/SaaS, Education, or Something else). Your agent uses this to introduce your business and answer accurately. Click **Continue**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/user-guides/quick-start/first-agent/business.png" alt="Business name and type step" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a handling mode">
    Pick how conversations are handled — you can change this anytime.

    - **AI Handling** *(recommended)* — your agent answers customers automatically, 24/7, and you can take over any conversation.
    - **Manual Handling** — you and your team reply to every message; the agent just collects them into one inbox.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/user-guides/quick-start/first-agent/handling-mode.png" alt="AI Handling vs Manual Handling step" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name your agent">
    Set the **agent name** customers see in the chat, and optionally note what it helps with. Click **Save agent**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/user-guides/quick-start/first-agent/agent-name.png" alt="Name your agent step" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your agent is created. Next, give it something to learn from.

---

## Step 2 — Add training data

Open **Data Sources** in the sidebar. This is where you add everything your agent learns from, and where you train it.

<Frame caption="The Data Sources hub — add sources, then train your agent.">
  <img src="/images/user-guides/quick-start/first-agent/data-sources.png" alt="Data Sources page with file upload, text, Q&A, and catalog sources" />
</Frame>

Pick whichever sources fit your business — you can combine several:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Files">
    Upload **PDF, DOC, TXT, or CSV** files — manuals, FAQs, product sheets, policies.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/user-guides/quick-start/first-agent/file-upload.png" alt="File Upload page" />
    </Frame>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Text Content">
    Paste text directly — great for quick knowledge‑base snippets without a file.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Website">
    Crawl your site or add specific page URLs — ideal for docs, blogs, and product pages.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Q&A Pairs">
    Add question‑and‑answer pairs for precise, controlled responses to common questions.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Catalog">
    Add your products so the agent can recommend items and answer price/variant questions. See [Product Catalog](/user-guides/catalog/introduction).
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### Train the agent

After adding sources, click **Train agent** (top‑right, or **Train now** in the banner). Training usually takes 2–5 minutes depending on how much data you added.

<Note>
  Whenever you add or change a source, the agent needs a retrain to learn it — a banner reminds you when training is pending.
</Note>

---

## Step 3 — Test your agent

Open **Test Agent** to chat with your agent in a live environment before deploying. Check its answers for accuracy, relevance, completeness, and tone.

<Frame caption="Chat with your agent and confirm it answers well before going live.">
  <img src="/images/user-guides/quick-start/first-agent/test-agent.png" alt="Test AI Agent page with chat and agent status" />
</Frame>

<Tip>
  Not happy with an answer? Add or refine a source (a Q&A pair is great for fixing a specific response), retrain, and test again.
</Tip>

---

## Step 4 — Deploy

Open **Deploy** to see every channel your agent can go live on — your website chat widget, a help page, WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram, Telegram, and more.

<Frame caption="The Deploy page — connect any channel.">
  <img src="/images/user-guides/deploy/all-channels.png" alt="Deploy page showing all channels" />
</Frame>

The fastest way to go live is the **Chat Widget** on your website. Click **Configure Widget**, set the name, welcome message, colors, and position, and watch the live preview.

<Frame caption="Configure the chat widget, with a live preview.">
  <img src="/images/user-guides/deploy/widget-config.png" alt="Chat Widget configuration with live preview" />
</Frame>

Then open the **Widget Script** tab and copy the snippet:

```html
<script>
    window.AIAgent = {
        agentId: 'your-agent-id'
    };
</script>
<script src="https://chatros.com/widget.min.js"></script>
```

<Warning>
  Copy the snippet from your own dashboard — it contains your agent's unique ID. The example above is illustrative.
</Warning>

Paste it into your website's HTML, just before the closing `</body>` tag, on every page where you want the widget. Save and publish, then reload your site — the chat bubble appears in the corner.

<Note>
  On **Shopify** or **WooCommerce**? You don't paste code — install through the ChatRos app/plugin instead. See [Shopify](/user-guides/deploy/shopify) and [WooCommerce](/user-guides/deploy/woocommerce).
</Note>

You've created, trained, tested, and deployed your first AI Agent. It's now live and ready to help your visitors 24/7.
