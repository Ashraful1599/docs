---

Connect your Facebook Page and your agent will automatically respond to Messenger conversations with AI. Setup is a one‑click Facebook sign‑in — no webhooks or tokens to configure.

## Before you connect

- You have a **Facebook Page** and you're an **admin** of it.
- You know which Page your agent should reply from (you'll pick it during setup if you manage more than one).

## Connect Messenger

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Messenger channel">
    From your agent, go to **Deploy → Integration Channels → Messenger**.

    <Frame caption="The Messenger connect panel.">
      <img src="/images/user-guides/deploy/messenger-panel.png" alt="Messenger integration panel with Connect with Facebook button" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click “Connect with Facebook”">
    You'll be redirected to Facebook to sign in and grant permission to manage your Page and read/send messages.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Grant permissions">
    Approve the requested permissions. When you're done, Facebook sends you back to ChatRos.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your Page (if prompted)">
    If you manage more than one Page, pick the one this agent should respond from, then click **Connect This Page**.

    {/* SHOT: deploy/messenger-select-page.png | The "Choose a Facebook Page" selection screen */}
    <Note>📸 Screenshot: the **Choose a Facebook Page** selection screen *(to be added)*.</Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Done">
    You'll see a green **Connected** card showing your Page name and ID. Your agent now replies to Messenger automatically — no further setup needed.

    {/* SHOT: deploy/messenger-connected.png | The connected Messenger card */}
    <Note>📸 Screenshot: the connected Messenger card *(to be added)*.</Note>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What your agent can do on Messenger

- **Reply automatically** to Messenger conversations on your Page.
- **Send and receive images**.
- **Show a typing indicator** and report **delivery and read receipts**.

## Manage or disconnect

Return to **Deploy → Messenger** to view the connected Page or click **Disconnect**. Disconnecting stops your agent from handling that Page's Messenger conversations.
