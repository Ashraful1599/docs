---
title: "Shopify"
description: "Add your agent to a Shopify store so it looks up live products and order status."
---

Connect your Shopify store and your agent can answer real questions about your catalog and orders — *"Do you have this in medium?"*, *"Where's my order?"* — using live, read‑only data from Shopify. You also install the on‑site chat widget through Shopify's theme editor, with no code.

<Note>
  ChatRos reads your products and order status **read‑only**. It never modifies your store, products, or orders.
</Note>

## Step 1 — Install the ChatRos Shopify app

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install from the Shopify App Store">
    Install the **ChatRos** app from the [Shopify App Store](https://apps.shopify.com/) (or from your Shopify admin → **Apps**). Installing it links your store to your ChatRos workspace automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve the connection">
    Follow Shopify's install prompts and approve the requested permissions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Step 2 — Connect the store to your agent

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Shopify integration">
    In ChatRos, go to your agent → **Integrations → Shopify**.

    <Frame caption="The Shopify integration page.">
      <img src="/images/user-guides/deploy/shopify-connect.png" alt="Shopify integration page with Connect Shopify store button" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click “Connect Shopify store”">
    This links the Shopify store already connected to your workspace to *this* agent. Once connected, you'll see a green **● Connected** badge with your store URL.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If ChatRos says no store is linked yet, install the ChatRos app from your Shopify admin first (Step 1), then come back and click **Connect Shopify store**.
</Tip>

## Step 3 — Show the chat widget on your storefront

Shopify stores install the chat widget through Shopify's **theme app embed** — you never paste a script.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the theme editor">
    From the Chat Widget's **Script** tab in ChatRos, use the button to open your Shopify theme editor with the ChatRos embed pre‑selected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable the ChatRos app embed">
    Toggle the **ChatRos** app embed on, then **Save**. The widget appears on your storefront automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

{/* SHOT: deploy/shopify-app-embed.png | Shopify theme editor with the ChatRos app embed toggle */}

## Verify it works

Open your storefront and ask the agent about a product you sell, or an order number. It should answer using live data from your store.

## Manage or disconnect

Return to **Integrations → Shopify** to see the connected store or click **Disconnect**.
