title: "Instagram"

---

Connect your Instagram Business or Creator account and your agent will automatically read and reply to Instagram direct messages. Setup uses the same one‑click Facebook sign‑in as Messenger.

## Before you connect

- You have an **Instagram Business** or **Creator** account.
- That account is **linked to a Facebook Page** you administer. (Instagram messaging is managed through the connected Facebook Page.)

<Tip>
  Not sure if your account is a Business/Creator account? Open the Instagram app → **Settings → Account type and tools** to switch. Then link it to your Facebook Page from the same settings area.
</Tip>

## Connect Instagram

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Instagram channel">
    From your agent, go to **Deploy → Integration Channels → Instagram**.

    <Frame caption="The Instagram connect panel.">
      <img src="/images/user-guides/deploy/instagram-panel.png" alt="Connect Instagram panel with Connect with Facebook button" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click “Connect with Facebook”">
    You'll be redirected to Meta to sign in with your Facebook Business account and approve access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Grant permissions">
    Approve the requested permissions. Meta then returns you to ChatRos.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Done">
    You'll see an **Instagram Channel Created!** confirmation with your account username and ID. Your agent now handles Instagram DMs automatically.

    {/* SHOT: deploy/instagram-connected.png | The "Instagram Channel Created!" success state */}
    <Note>📸 Screenshot: the **Instagram Channel Created!** success state *(to be added)*.</Note>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What your agent can do on Instagram

- **Reply automatically** to Instagram DMs.
- **Send and receive images**.
- **Show a typing indicator** and report **delivery and read receipts**.

## Manage or disconnect

Return to **Deploy → Instagram** to view the connected account or click **Disconnect** to stop handling its messages.
